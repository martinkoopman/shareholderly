# Vanguard — Proxy Voting Policy for U.S. Portfolio Companies

**Effective January 2026.** Adapted from *Proxy voting policy for U.S. portfolio companies*
published by Vanguard Portfolio Management, LLC / Vanguard Fiduciary Trust Company /
Vanguard Global Advisers, LLC. Source: corporate.vanguard.com/.../vpm-proxy-voting-policy-us.pdf

Each ballot item is mapped to one of the proposal types below, and the listed rules
are applied in order. The first rule that fires determines the vote. Vanguard's
underlying policy is heavily case-by-case; the rules below codify the thresholds
that Vanguard states explicitly.

---

## 1. Director Elections

- **AGAINST** any director who attended less than **75%** of board or committee meetings (in aggregate) in the prior year, unless an extenuating circumstance is disclosed or they have served less than one year.
- **AGAINST** any non-independent director who sits on the **audit, compensation, or nominating/governance committee** of a non-controlled company.
- **AGAINST** any director who serves on **more than 4 public-company boards** (overboarded). Vanguard applies a tighter limit of 2 public-company boards for directors who are also public-company executives; the recommender does not currently distinguish.
- Otherwise vote **FOR**.

> Note: Vanguard does **not** impose a tenure cap. Tenure is left to the board.

## 2. Ratification of Auditor

- **AGAINST** when **non-audit fees exceed audit fees**, unless the company's disclosure makes clear that the non-audit fees do not impair auditor independence. The recommender uses non-audit > audit as the trigger because the case-by-case overlay isn't automatable.
- Otherwise vote **FOR**.

> Note: Vanguard does **not** apply a fixed auditor-tenure threshold.

## 3. Say-on-Pay (Advisory Vote on Executive Compensation)

- **AGAINST** if pay-for-performance is **not aligned** over the trailing 3-year period (CAP vs. TSR vs. peers).
- Otherwise vote **FOR**, consistent with Vanguard's case-by-case approach.

> Note: Vanguard does **not** use a CEO-pay-ratio threshold. Higher-concern situations Vanguard flags but the recommender does not yet score: LTI <50% of total pay, LTI performance period <3 years, target total pay above peer-group median, plan targets reset or retested.

## 4. Equity Compensation Plans

- **AGAINST** if total potential dilution (across all stock-based plans) exceeds **20%** of shares outstanding.
- **AGAINST** if the 3-year average annual equity grant rate exceeds **4%** of shares outstanding.
- Otherwise vote **FOR**.

> Note: Vanguard also opposes plans that permit option repricing without shareholder approval, reload options, or evergreen share replenishment. The recommender does not yet detect these features.

## 5. Shareholder Proposals — Environmental & Social

Vanguard evaluates E&S proposals case by case. Support is more likely when the
proposal addresses a disclosure shortcoming relative to widely accepted frameworks
(e.g., ISSB), reflects an industry-specific materiality-driven approach, and is not
overly prescriptive (does not dictate strategy, day-to-day operations, time frames,
or costs).

The recommender approximates this with a topic allow-list:

- **FOR** climate-risk disclosure (TCFD/ISSB-aligned).
- **FOR** human-rights impact assessments in high-risk operations.
- **FOR** workforce diversity reporting (EEO-1-style disclosure).
- **ABSTAIN** otherwise — flag for manual case-by-case review.

## 6. Shareholder Proposals — Governance

- **FOR** proposals to eliminate **supermajority voting requirements**.
- **FOR** proposals to allow shareholders to call a **special meeting** at the **10%** ownership threshold or higher.
- **AGAINST** proposals to **classify the board** (staggered terms); Vanguard supports declassification.
- **AGAINST** proposals to require an **independent board chair** (separate CEO and chair). Vanguard believes board leadership structure is the board's purview; exceptions exist where there are significant concerns about board independence or effectiveness.

## 7. Default

- If a ballot item does not match any of the above, vote **ABSTAIN** and flag for manual review.
